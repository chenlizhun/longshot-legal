Help & Support

Stitch screenshots

Capture a sequence with roughly 20–50% overlap and select the images in order. Review the result and adjust seams before exporting.

Use a screen recording

Start iPhone screen recording, scroll steadily in one direction, then import the recording. Rapid jumps, reverse scrolling and changing pages can affect stitching.

Free export allowance

3 on the first-use day, 2 on the second day, then 1 per day, calculated by local calendar date. Skipped dates count; unused exports do not carry forward. Free exports include an edutogether watermark.

Restore Pro

Use the same Apple Account used for the original purchase and tap Restore Purchase on the Pro screen. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If it cannot be restored, check your purchase history and connection, then contact support.

Refunds

Request a refund through Apple's Report a Problem service. Apple handles and decides refund requests.

Permissions and deletion

Manage Photos permissions in iOS Settings. Deleting app history does not remove copies already saved to Photos, Files or other services.
