Privacy Policy

eScrollShot (e滚动截屏) for iOS · Updated October 31, 2026; applies to build 2026103101 and later

At a glance: Images, recordings and text are processed on your device. The developer does not receive that content or collect app usage analytics. Apple purchases, sharing and support email involve the services described below.

1. Developer and scope

This policy is provided by chenlizhun. The app has no developer-operated account system and requires no registration or app login. This policy does not replace the policies of websites you visit, Apple, your email provider or services you choose when sharing.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Your content: Imported screenshots, recordings, text and generated images are used locally for stitching, rendering, editing and export. They are not uploaded to a developer server.
  • History: Images, thumbnails, titles, timestamps and dimensions are kept in local app storage for viewing and re-export.
  • Preferences and entitlements: Language, appearance, quality settings, recording preferences, first-use date, daily free export counters and Pro status are stored locally to operate the app. The language preference is also saved in the shared App Group so that the share extension can use it.
  • Share extension: Incoming text and images are temporarily stored in an on-device App Group container shared with the main app.

3. Photos permissions and recordings

  • Read access: Used to import selected images or recordings and to find recent screenshots or recordings. With limited access, only content you have allowed is available.
  • Add access: Used to save generated images. Add-only permission does not itself permit reading existing photos.
  • Deleting a source recording: Happens only when you request deletion or enable the relevant preference. Automatic deletion occurs only after the result has been saved to Photos and is subject to system permissions and confirmation.

You can change or revoke permissions in iOS Settings. Denying access restricts the related import, save or delete features; text features that do not need photo read access remain available. You create recordings using system controls. The app does not independently record your screen in the background.

4. Network requests

The current version does not provide web capture or URL browsing. External privacy and terms links open through system services, whose providers process network requests.

5. Apple purchases, sharing and email support

  • Purchases and restoration: Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed through Apple StoreKit and the App Store. The app reads product information, verified transactions and entitlements returned by Apple to display pricing and unlock features. The developer does not obtain your card details, Apple Account password or full payment credentials through the app. Apple may provide the developer with sales, settlement or aggregate reports.
  • Sharing: Saving to Photos or Files, copying, or using the system share sheet sends the selected content to the destination or service you choose. Further processing depends on that service and your device settings.
  • Support email: If you email the developer, your email address, message and attachments are received and used to respond to your request and related support communications. Do not send passwords, payment details or unrelated sensitive information.

6. Advertising, analytics and tracking

The app includes no third-party advertising, behavioral analytics or crash-collection SDKs. It does not use IDFA for cross-app tracking or send your content or usage statistics to a developer server. Websites, Apple, email and sharing services handle their own communications under their policies.

7. Retention, deletion and backups

  • Local history remains until you delete it, it is removed under the app's local history capacity policy, or the app's data is erased. You can delete one or several history items or clear history.
  • Deleting the app normally removes its local app data. The iOS “Offload App” option retains data and differs from deletion. System backups, restoration, iCloud Photos and file synchronization are controlled by iOS and your settings; the developer does not operate cloud content storage for this app.
  • Copies saved or shared elsewhere must be managed at their destination. Deleting app history or the app does not delete them.
  • You may request deletion of information supplied through support email by contacting the address below. Requests are handled in light of ongoing support needs and records that must lawfully be retained.

8. Minors

The app is not specifically directed at children and does not intentionally collect children's personal information. Follow the App Store age rating and use system parental controls where appropriate. Contact the developer if you believe a child has supplied personal information through support email.

9. This website

The developer's international website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not include registration, online payment, advertising, analytics scripts or contact forms. Your browser makes requests to the hosting provider when you visit; that provider may process network and security information under its own policy. External links and email open the services you choose. The separate China website may use server access and error logs for operation, security and troubleshooting, as described on that site.

10. Changes and contact

Updates are dated on this page. If new features change information collection, use or sharing, the policy will be updated and authorization obtained where required. Continued use does not replace explicit consent where the law requires it.
